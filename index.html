About me

Hi! Good to see you here. :)

This is Jaime, a novel researcher finishing Master studies, under the supervision of Daniel Martin and Ana Serrano. My contributions to the field of Extended Reality (XR) include the analysis of gaze and performance from people in immersive environments, based on factors like multisensory inputs, interaction with reality via passthrough, or cognitive load. My interests in XR include research on these topics and similar ones for comprehension of human attention and insights in how users behave in immersive environments. I'm about to finish my Master studies, and I'm looking forward to set my path for PhD studies in the next course.

How to pronounce my name: hi + meh. You can also check it here.
