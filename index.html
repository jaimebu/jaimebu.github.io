About me

Hi! Good to see you here. :)

This is Jaime, a novel researcher starting doctoral studies, under the supervision of Prof. Daniel Martin and Prof. Ana Serrano. My contributions to the field of Extended Reality (XR) include the analysis of gaze and performance from people in immersive environments, based on factors like multisensory inputs, interaction with reality via passthrough, or cognitive load. My interests include research on these topics and similar ones for comprehension of human attention and insights in how users behave in immersive environments.
